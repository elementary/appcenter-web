<p>
      For those who prefer to avoid using command lines, or who find <code>df</code> output hard to read.
      Quickly visualize the space used and available on your storage volumes.
    </p>
<p>
      This simple utility is an alternative to KDiskFree that shows your volumes gives some information about them.
    </p>
<p>
      It also allows you to mount or unmount volumes. If a NTFS volumes fails to mount, you can ignore the errors and force mounting it (at your own risks).
    </p>
<p><em>"Espace Libre" is French for "Free Space"</em></p>
